Sidequestor Field Guide
Two short chapters for keeping your quests moving.
Your folder, running as a service
Sidequestor is for people who want to expose a local working directory as a service that stays awake. It can keep doing work, help colleagues, notice new replies, and continue a mission while you are away.
The golden rule
Want to work interactively with a quest or anything in its folder? Go straight to your Terminal. That is your workshop.
The quest loop
1
Create
Describe the mission, people involved, and what “done” should look like.
2
Watch
Tripwires listen for Slack, email, schedule, or repo activity.
3
Act
The local agent does the next step, records what happened, and can add new channels, tickets, and emails to watch.
4
Continue
The quest remembers its state and waits for the next useful signal.
Choose your screen
Terminal = the workshop
Create or edit files, inspect quest state, and work directly with your local agent here.
Dashboard = the map
Scan activity, open a quest’s story, and review work in an easy-to-read view. Quests themselves are created in the Terminal. The files remain the source of truth.
Cast a tiny spell from Slack
Add a configured reaction to any Slack message. Sidequestor sees it on a later tick, picks up the thread context, and performs that one-off action for you.
No quest required
Reaction jobs use a fast path for ad hoc work. A quest is only needed when the job turns into ongoing follow-up.
